# SpudBoost

**Potato PC? Boost it.** One-click Windows and game optimizations with full undo.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/FlopzYT/SpudBoost/releases/latest)**:

- **SpudBoost-Setup-x.y.z.exe**: the installer (recommended). SpudBoost updates itself after that; just click **Update now** when it tells you there's a new version.
- **SpudBoost-x.y.z-portable.zip**: unzip and run, nothing to install.

### "Windows protected your PC"?

SpudBoost isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
If your browser blocks the download, choose **Keep** (in Edge: the **…** menu next to the download).

## What it does

- **One-click Boost** with a Spud Score that shows how game-ready your PC is
- **60+ tweaks** for performance, gaming, input, Windows clutter and privacy, each with one-click undo
- **Game Boost**: raises your game's priority and keeps background apps out of the way while you play
- **Game library** with Boost & Play and game-specific tweaks for Steam, Epic and Minecraft games
- **Fortnite potato graphics**, custom crosshairs, Roblox / Minecraft / CS2 graphics settings
- **Cleaner, debloater, network tweaks, FPS benchmark, hardware monitor** and in-game stats overlay
- **Profiles** you can save, switch between and share

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Some tweaks need administrator rights; SpudBoost asks when they do.
